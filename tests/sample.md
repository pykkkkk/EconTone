Using provincial panel data for 2008-2019, this paper investigates the impact of high-speed rail openings on local house prices and industrial structure. Employing a fixed-effects model, we find that HSR openings significantly increase house prices and promote industrial upgrading. Heterogeneity analysis shows that the effect is more pronounced in larger cities. Robustness checks confirm the findings. These results highlight the importance of transport infrastructure for regional development and offer important policy implications.
